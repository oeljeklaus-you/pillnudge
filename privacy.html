Privacy Policy for PillNudge

Effective Date: May 18, 2026

Overview

PillNudge is designed as a simple personal medication reminder and dose tracking app.

Data Collection

PillNudge does not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

Medication names, schedules, reminder times, and dose logs are stored locally on your device unless a future version clearly states otherwise and asks for your permission.

Health and Medical Information

PillNudge is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, dosage recommendations, or emergency services. The app is intended only to help users organize personal medication reminders and dose logs.

Notifications

PillNudge may request notification permission in order to send medication reminders. Notification permission can be managed in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

PillNudge does not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

Children’s Privacy

PillNudge does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
